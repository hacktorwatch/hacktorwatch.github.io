THE WEBSITE / DATA HANDLING

Privacy, in plain language.

This page describes browsing the website and contacting the Hacktor Watch team by email. Firmware you choose to run on a watch has its own behaviour.

BrowsingEmail enquiriesYour choicesContact

Browsing the site.

The website serves its images, fonts and scripts from the same site. It does not add analytics, advertising trackers, cookies or browser storage for tracking.

When deployed to GitHub Pages, GitHub processes hosting request information under its own privacy statement. Links to GitHub and other external websites take you to those services, where their own policies apply.

Contacting us by email.

The website provides an email link and a button to copy contact@hacktorwatch.com. The email link opens your email application; you decide whether to send a message. There is no signup form or registration database on the website.

If you email us, the Hacktor Watch team receives your sender address and the information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to respond to your enquiry and any related follow-up.

Email is handled by the mail services used by you and the project. Sending an enquiry does not automatically subscribe you to a mailing list.

Your choices.

You can browse the project without contacting us. Please include only the details needed for your enquiry.

To ask about correspondence you have sent us, or request correction or removal of information, email contact@hacktorwatch.com.

Privacy questions.

contact@hacktorwatch.com
